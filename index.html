About STEM Classrooms

STEM classrooms provide an interactive learning space integrating Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics, preparing students for a technology-driven future.

Why STEM?

A Hands-On Approach to Learning

STEM education fosters curiosity, critical thinking, and problem-solving through real-world applications.

  • Hands-on Learning: Engage in experiments, coding, and engineering projects.
  • Problem-Solving: Develop creativity and critical thinking skills.
  • Collaboration: Work in teams to enhance communication and teamwork.
  • Technology Integration: Utilize digital tools, simulations, and robotics.
  • Inquiry-Based Learning: Encourage students to explore and innovate.

Empowering Communities Through Banking

Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC, Sri Lanka’s largest private bank, plays a vital role in economic growth by offering comprehensive financial services. With nearly 100 years of experience, it leads in digital banking, corporate solutions, and financial inclusion.

The STEM classroom initiative bridges the digital divide, equipping underprivileged schools with modern technology to foster the next generation of innovators and problem solvers.

Commercial Bank

STEM Initiative

Investing in Education for a Brighter Future

Beyond banking, Commercial Bank actively supports education by ensuring technology access for students. The STEM project has provided digital resources to over 170 schools nationwide.

Investing in education today will create a skilled workforce tomorrow. Through initiatives like this, the goal is to empower Sri Lanka’s youth with the tools needed for success in the digital era.

Commercial Bank

Education Support

Ensuring Impactful Learning

The Career Guidance Unit (CGU) of the University of Colombo plays a crucial role in evaluating the effectiveness of donated STEM classrooms. Through systematic assessments, it ensures the resources are utilized to their fullest potential.

The Service-Learning course provides hands-on experience for students, fostering a deeper understanding of STEM education’s impact in underprivileged communities.

Career Guidance Unit

University of Colombo

Bridging the Gap Between Education and Industry

CGU actively collaborates with schools and organizations to align STEM education with industry needs. The mission is to create a seamless transition from academia to the workforce.

By analyzing student progress and feedback, CGU continuously improves the STEM curriculum, ensuring that graduates are equipped with skills demanded by the modern job market.
